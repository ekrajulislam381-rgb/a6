1. General Haute Couture Information

The treatises, mathematical textile physics models, and patternmaking monographs published on https://moiretrendaxis.com are shared for educational, historical, and aesthetic enrichment. While compiled by master sartorial technologists, this material is presented without warranties of commercial universality.

2. Natural Silk & Hand-Woven Variance Advisory

Authentic moiré silk faille is an organic artisanal medium woven on traditional Jacquard looms and finished with hydraulic steel calenders. Natural slubs, slight weft variations, and subtle shifts in optical interference wave angles are inherent characteristics of genuine hand-finished silk and do not constitute manufacturing flaws.

3. Architectural Garment Wear & Motion Advisory

Certain high-concept runway silhouettes incorporate rigid internal titanium stays and exaggerated cantilevered projections designed for editorial presentation and black-tie galas. Wearers should exercise mindfulness when sitting, entering vehicles, or navigating confined spaces.
