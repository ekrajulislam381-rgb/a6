Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Last Modified: March 9, 2026

1. Introduction & Scope

Moiré Trend Axis ("we", "our", or "us"), operating our flagship salon and workshop at 520 West 28th Street, Chelsea Arts District, New York, NY 10001, recognizes the paramount importance of your personal confidentiality. This Privacy Policy sets forth our principles and protocols regarding the collection, handling, processing, and protection of personal data gathered via https://moiretrendaxis.com, private appointment bookings, and bespoke commission communications.

2. Categories of Information Collected

In delivering bespoke haute couture tailoring, we collect and process the following classifications of data:

  • Client Identification & Contact Data: Name, residential address, telephone number, and electronic correspondence details provided during appointment registration.
  • Anatomical Biometric Metrics: Precise three-dimensional laser body scan metrics, posture coordinates, and garment measurements required exclusively to draft and construct bespoke made-to-measure patterns.
  • Transactional & Payment Records: Billing addresses, deposit confirmations, and payment verifications processed via PCI-DSS encrypted payment gateways. We do not retain raw payment card figures on local systems.
  • Technical Telemetry: Anonymized analytical logs, browser signatures, and interaction timelines monitored via analytical cookies (G-0LY0HY7L01).

3. Legal Bedrock for Data Processing

Our data processing operations are anchored upon established legal bedrocks recognized under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and CalOPPA:

  • Contractual Execution: Constructing and delivering custom tailored garments per commission covenants.
  • Legitimate Interests: Protecting our intellectual pattern drafting algorithms and optimizing atelier appointment logistics.
  • Statutory Compliance: Satisfying tax, accounting, and commercial luxury sales regulations.

4. Data Disclosure & Custody

We do not sell, license, or barter your personal records or anatomical metrics. Disclosures are strictly confined to vetted operational affiliates (e.g. encrypted cloud database hosts, international logistics couriers delivering archival caskets, and secure transaction gateways) bound by binding non-disclosure agreements.

5. Your Privacy Rights

You hold rights to review, rectify, download, or demand erasure of your personal data and anatomical scans at any point. To submit a request, contact our Privacy Compliance Officer at atelier@moiretrendaxis.com or via +1-888-692-7483.
