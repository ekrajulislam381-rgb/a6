Cookie Policy

Last Modified: March 9, 2026

1. Understanding Cookie Technology

Cookies are minute alphanumeric data packets placed on your computing terminal to retain browsing preferences, optimize interactive performance, and provide anonymous usage metrics.

2. Categories of Cookies Deployed

  • Essential Operations Cookies: Indispensable for secure site navigation, CSRF prevention, and appointment form preservation.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: We utilize Google Analytics (G-0LY0HY7L01) to evaluate visitor flows, device display resolutions, and navigational responsiveness.
  • Functional Setting Cookies: Memorize your chosen measurement unit preferences (metric vs imperial) during browsing.

3. Browser Cookie Controls

You may elect to modify, restrict, or disable cookies via your browser's security settings. Disabling essential cookies may impair the interactive appointment scheduling portal.
